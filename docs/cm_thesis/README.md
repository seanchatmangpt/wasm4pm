## Directory Structure and Plan

This directory (`docs/cm_thesis/`) contains the structural scaffolding for a PhD thesis focused on stress-testing and analyzing the theoretical and practical limits of the `wasm4pm` architecture. 

The thesis is divided into chapters that mirror the combinatorial maximalist research plan.

### Core Files
*   **`README.md`**: This document. An index and roadmap for the thesis.
*   **`METHODOLOGY.md`**: Outlines the experimental setup, hardware bounds, and definitions of "combinatorial explosion" within the context of process mining.
*   **`BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`**: The foundational research backbone, literature review, and academic positioning spanning seven disciplinary streams.
*   **`CHAPTER_1_ALGORITHMIC_MATRIX.md`**: Investigates the cross-product of branchless primitives and algorithmic execution profiles.
*   **`CHAPTER_2_STATE_SPACE.md`**: Maps the 8-dimensional Reinforcement Learning state machine and recovery limits.
*   **`CHAPTER_3_OBJECT_CENTRIC.md`**: Explores the multi-dimensional N-partite graph limits of OCEL and POWL v2.
*   **`CHAPTER_4_ADVERSARIAL_BOUNDARIES.md`**: Details the cryptographic (BLAKE3) and adversarial probe Cartesian products.
*   **`CHAPTER_5_COMPUTE_CONTINUUM.md`**: Analyzes these extremes across IoT, Edge, Browser, and Cloud boundaries.
*   **`CONCLUSION.md`**: Final synthesis of the architectural limits.
