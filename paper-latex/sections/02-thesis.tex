The ``Blue-Ocean Thesis'' postulates that mechanical intelligence---the deterministic, receipted application of advanced calculus and combinatorial search across a bounded space---yields significantly higher autonomic value than stochastic, unbounded general intelligence (AGI) applied to process execution. 

First-mile intelligence restricts operations to:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Mathematical Rigor}: Every operation must reduce to an algebraic mapping or discrete calculus transformation.
    \item \textbf{Microsecond Bounds}: Operations must execute in sub-millisecond latencies, rendering them suitable for high-frequency edge execution.
    \item \textbf{Total Auditability}: Execution leaves a cryptographic artifact (receipt) binding the input, the operation hash, and the output state.
\end{enumerate}
