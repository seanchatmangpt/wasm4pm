## 4. What NOT to Do

- **Don't consolidate the concept-drift/anomaly duplication (6+ implementations) this cycle.** It's real (L effort / M impact, 0.67 leverage) but spans the WASM boundary and two TS layers — high risk of breaking cross-language contracts for a single sprint's work.
- **Don't chase the CLI/kernel 11-file refactor (parse/execute/format split).** Genuine pattern, but L effort against M impact (0.67) — the file count alone makes this a multi-week initiative, not an 80/20 pick.
- **Don't try to fix branch hygiene by deleting branches without addressing the daily `integration/merge-all-*` funneling habit.** The scan explicitly flags this as the compounding root cause (0.5–1.0 leverage on the branch cleanup alone) — cleaning without changing the workflow just refills the queue.
- **Don't spend effort on the unconfirmed-overlap files** (`automl.rs` vs optimization/, `cuts.rs`/`wf_to_powl.rs`, GPU LinUCB path) — each is explicitly flagged as unverified overlap or unconfirmed demand (0.5 leverage), lower confidence than the verified duplication items above them.
