\chapter*{Acknowledgments}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Acknowledgments}

This work stands on the shoulders of the process mining discipline established by
Wil M.~P.~van der Aalst, whose insistence that conformance between declared models
and observed event logs is not a discrepancy but a defect provided the theoretical
and methodological foundation for the Object-Level Diagnostic Inference Architecture
described here. The van~der~Aalst constitution---\emph{trust only event evidence
that can be mined into a conforming object-centric process}---pervades every layer
of this design.

Deep acknowledgment is owed to the original researchers whose work this architecture
honors: Edward~H.~Shortliffe and the MYCIN team, who demonstrated that structured
knowledge could reason reliably under uncertainty; Allen~Newell and Herbert~A.~Simon,
whose General Problem Solver first articulated the architecture of symbolic search;
Lee~D.~Erman and Victor~R.~Lesser for Hearsay-II's blackboard model of cooperative
problem solving; Bruce~G.~Buchanan and Edward~A.~Feigenbaum for DENDRAL's demonstration
that domain knowledge encodes discovery; Joseph~Weizenbaum for ELIZA's uncomfortable
lesson about the boundary between language and understanding; John~Alan~Robinson for
the resolution principle that made machine inference tractable;
Janet~Kolodner, Agnar~Aamodt, and Enric~Plaza for case-based reasoning;
Richard~E.~Fikes and Nils~J.~Nilsson for STRIPS; John~E.~Laird,
Allen~Newell, and Paul~S.~Rosenbloom for SOAR; Roger~C.~Schank for
Conceptual Dependency; Karl~R.~Popper for the logical structure of refutation;
Alain~Colmerauer and his colleagues for Prolog; Terry~Winograd for SHRDLU;
Kenneth~M.~Colby for PARRY; and Patrick~H.~Winston for structural learning from
examples. Each of these systems left a visible seam in OLDIA's breed taxonomy.

The author is grateful to the open-source communities whose infrastructure makes
a deterministic, auditable architecture feasible at the scale described here:
the \textbf{WebAssembly} Working Group and the contributors to
\texttt{wasm-bindgen} and \texttt{wasm-pack}, whose portable binary format
provides the isolation boundary between the inference kernel and its host;
the \textbf{BLAKE3} team, whose cryptographic hash function supplies the
receipt-chain integrity guarantees that make replay and conformance auditing
meaningful; and the \textbf{Rust} language community, whose ownership model and
compilation guarantees eliminate an entire class of runtime defects before any
event log is ever written.

Any errors of judgment, gaps in conformance, or violations of the van~der~Aalst
constitution that remain in this work are the sole responsibility of the author.
