# Admissions & Faculty Handbook

## 1. Program Mission
The Ph.D. program at the Institute for Advanced Process Studies exists to push the physical and theoretical limits of autonomous systems via combinatorial maximalism. We seek candidates ready to build, break, and mathematically prove the bounds of nanosecond process intelligence architectures.

## 2. Admissions Prerequisites
To be considered for the program, applicants must demonstrate:
1.  **Academic Baseline:** An M.S. in Computer Science, Systems Engineering, Mathematics, or equivalent demonstrable industry experience.
2.  **Systems Programming:** Advanced proficiency in Systems-level programming (Rust, C++, or Zig). Candidates must understand linear memory management and compiler optimizations.
3.  **Theoretical Logic:** Foundational knowledge of First-Order Logic and discrete mathematics. Experience with logic programming (Prolog, Datalog, ASP) is highly preferred.
4.  **Process Science Background:** Familiarity with process mining concepts (Petri Nets, Alpha Miner) and distributed systems architecture (Actor Model).

## 3. Hardware & Infrastructure Provisioning
The Institute provides the following infrastructure for all admitted candidates to execute the "Compute Continuum" research:
*   **Cloud Tier:** $10,000 annual AWS/GCP credits for deploying native MCPP orchestrator clusters and saturating bandwidth.
*   **Edge Tier:** A standardized "Civic Edge Kit" containing 5x Raspberry Pi 5s (or equivalent ARM64 SBCs) pre-configured with Wasmtime and localized battery backups to test network partition resilience.
*   **Browser Tier:** Automated headless testing environments configured across all major browser engines (V8, SpiderMonkey, JavaScriptCore) to measure WASM linear memory boundaries.

## 4. Publication Requirements for Graduation
To maintain the rigor dictated by our Design Science Research (DSR) methodology, candidates must successfully peer-review their artifacts. 

**Prior to defending their dissertation, candidates must have:**
*   **Requirement 1 (Algorithm/Architecture):** One paper accepted as a primary author at a top-tier technical conference (e.g., PLDI, POPL, or specialized tracks at ICPM) proving the mathematical safety or performance boundary of their WASM/Prolog8 kernel enhancement.
*   **Requirement 2 (Application/Socio-Technical):** One paper accepted at a systems or process science conference (e.g., BPM, CAiSE, or equivalent MIS journals) demonstrating the real-world deployment, OCPM N-dimensional analysis, or adversarial testing of the `MCPP` ecosystem.

## 5. Committee Structure
A successful dissertation defense committee must consist of at least three faculty members representing the core ecosystem pillars:
1.  *Chair:* Process Intelligence / Autonomous Systems.
2.  *Member:* Cryptography / Bounded Logic / Admissibility.
3.  *Member:* Socio-Technical Systems / Systems Engineering.
