%% Abstract — The Periodic Table of Reason

Between 1956 and 1990, classical artificial intelligence produced a generation of landmark
architectures: \textsc{Mycin} diagnosed infectious disease with certainty factors,
\textsc{Strips} planned robotic action through goal regression, \textsc{Soar} unified
cognition through production rules and chunking, \textsc{Hearsay} coordinated speech
understanding through a blackboard, \textsc{Prolog} derived conclusions through resolution
over Horn clauses, CBR retrieved and adapted prior cases, GPS searched means--ends spaces,
\textsc{Dendral} proposed molecular structures through heuristic chemistry, and \textsc{Eliza}
reflected natural language through pattern-matching scripts. Each architecture was
epistemically rigorous within its own paradigm: it embodied a principled theory of how
knowledge is represented, updated, and discharged. None was operationally falsifiable. No
architecture shipped a proof that its runtime execution conformed to its declared inference
model; no architecture emitted a tamper-evident receipt linking input evidence to output
conclusion; no architecture produced an object-centric event log that could be replayed
against a process model under token-based conformance checking. The AI winter that followed
was not a failure of ideas. It was a failure of proof discipline.

This dissertation closes that gap through three contributions. First, we construct
\emph{complete, unfakeable oracle test suites} for all thirteen symbolic reasoning
architectures — \textsc{Mycin}, \textsc{Strips}, \textsc{Soar}, \textsc{Hearsay},
\textsc{Prolog}, CBR, GPS, \textsc{Dendral}, \textsc{Eliza}, and four
autoinstinct breeds (Vision, Semantics, Neurosis, Learning) — producing 483 tests with
zero failures and L0 wrapper conformance fitness $= 1.0$ for all thirteen breeds (L1
breed-inference step coverage $= 1.0$ for three flagship breeds; token-replay alignment
fitness deferred to proof-pack v2). Second, we define the
\emph{Epistemological Space} $\Ei = \mathbb{R}^8$ in which each breed occupies a unique
coordinate vector, prove that the thirteen breeds constitute thirteen basis objects in a typed admissibility category
over $\Ei$, and show that together they span the symbolic
cognition subspace of $\Ei$. Third, we introduce the \emph{Breed Validation Certificate}
$V(\Breed) = 1$ as the fourth constraint of the Universal Causal Equation: a breed is
admissible as a component of a manufactured intelligence pipeline if and only if it passes
oracle certification, OCEL process conformance, BLAKE3 receipt integrity, and
bit-exact determinism simultaneously.

Orthogonally, a \emph{Speed Phase Transition Theorem} shows that sub-microsecond
WebAssembly execution latency does not merely accelerate classical reasoning — it
\emph{enables qualitatively new capabilities} that were architecturally impossible for the
original investigators. Below the phase-transition threshold $\tau^* \approx 1\,\mu\text{s}$,
ensemble Bayesian analysis across all thirteen breeds becomes feasible in interactive time;
Pareto-frontier planning over uncertainty--depth trade-offs becomes a real-time operation;
and adversarial robustness auditing — systematic injection of malformed inputs to verify
rejection — becomes a continuous background process rather than an offline batch procedure.
The implementation is \textsc{partial-alive}: 483 tests, 0 failures, L0 wrapper fitness
$= 1.0$ for all thirteen breeds, L1 step coverage $= 1.0$ for three flagship breeds, and a BLAKE3 receipt chain covering \texttt{input\_hash} (emitted at the WASM
boundary in \texttt{cognition\_run()}) through \texttt{output\_hash} and
\texttt{ocel\_log} for every breed invocation in the validated corpus.
