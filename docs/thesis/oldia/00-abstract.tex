\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

\noindent
For seventy years, classical artificial intelligence produced architectures that were
theoretically elegant and operationally unverifiable.
MYCIN diagnosed infectious disease with demonstrable clinical accuracy yet left no
falsifiable execution trail.
SOAR modelled universal cognition with formal preference algebras yet offered no
mechanism for a downstream auditor to confirm that a particular run obeyed the
declared lifecycle.
Hearsay, GPS, STRIPS, DENDRAL, ELIZA, CBR, Prolog, and their successors shared the
same blind spot: correctness was a property of the design document, not of the
deployed runtime.
Every execution was, in the van der Aalst sense, a dark process---behaviour that
existed but could not be mined, replayed, or refused.

\medskip
\noindent
This thesis introduces \textbf{wasm4pm cognition breeds}: thirteen classical AI
architectures reimplemented as WebAssembly modules under a unified provability
contract.
The thirteen breeds are MYCIN (Shortliffe--Buchanan certainty-factor algebra),
Hearsay (KSAR blackboard with opportunistic scheduling), SOAR (full preference
algebra with subgoal impasse resolution), Case-Based Reasoning (4R cycle with
Jaccard similarity and BLAKE3 retained-case identity), Prolog (Robinson flat-term
unification with bounded SLD resolution), STRIPS (plan-soundness-verified forward
chaining), GPS (difference-coverage enforced means--ends analysis), DENDRAL
(forbidden-candidate elimination), ELIZA (pattern-action reflection), and four
Autoinstinct breeds---Vision, Semantics, Neurosis, and Learning---that express
emergent inference over perceptual and symbolic substrates.
Each breed is not merely implemented; it is \emph{validated, falsified, and
receipt-chained}.

\medskip
\noindent
The methodological contribution is an \textbf{OCEL 2.0 provability layer} wired at
the \texttt{run\_breed} chokepoint in \texttt{wasm.rs}.
Every breed execution emits an object-centric event log whose lifecycle phases are
checked against a statically declared \texttt{BreedLifecycleModel} (a deterministic
finite automaton over OCEL 2.0 activity labels) before any output is returned.
Non-conforming executions are refused, not merely flagged.
The \textbf{BLAKE3 receipt chain} extends provability from process to artefact:
\texttt{output\_hash = blake3(BreedOutput)}, \texttt{run\_id = blake3(breed
$\|$ output\_hash)}, and the OCEL log is embedded inside the output before hashing,
so the receipt attests to the process, not just the result.
Temporal conformance is enforced by strictly monotone \texttt{logical\_step}
attributes; wall-clock time is excluded from the hash domain to preserve
determinism under replay.

\medskip
\noindent
The test infrastructure comprises \textbf{655 tests with unfakeable oracles}:
480 Rust tests and 175 TypeScript tests, with zero failures recorded.
Rust coverage spans adversarial bypass attempts (58 tests), mathematical property
invariants (31 tests), formal SOAR/STRIPS/CBR invariants (23 tests), 13-breed
bit-exact double-run determinism harnesses (16 tests), and OCEL lifecycle
conformance (7 tests), among others.
Prolog was rewritten from scratch around Robinson unification and bounded SLD
resolution; the canonical grandparent test---\texttt{grandparent(?0,?2) :-
parent(?0,?1), parent(?1,?2)}---derives \texttt{carol} from a two-step chain and
is mathematically unfakeable without shared-variable unification.

\medskip
\noindent
Measured execution latency across the thirteen breeds runs from 2 to 50
microseconds on 2026 hardware, compiled to both \texttt{nodejs} and browser WASM
targets via wasm-pack.
The system operates as \textbf{civilization-scale auditable reasoning
infrastructure}: any institution that ingests a cognition breed receipt can
independently verify, replay, and mine the execution history as a first-class
process artefact.
The broader significance is architectural: OLDIA demonstrates that the gap between
theoretical AI correctness and operational verifiability is not a fundamental
limitation of classical reasoning but an engineering choice---one that can be
reversed by placing OCEL provability and receipt-chained falsifiability at the
execution boundary of every classical AI architecture deployed in the world.
