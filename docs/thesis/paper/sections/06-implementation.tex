We instantiate the framework in \emph{wasm4pm}~\cite{chatman_wasm4pm_2026}, an
open-source process mining platform. This section maps each formal component to
its concrete realisation and reports the evaluation evidence: determinism
guarantees, the proof-gate suite, binary footprints, and the operational
verification protocol.

\subsection{Architecture}

wasm4pm comprises two layers. The \textbf{core} is a Rust workspace compiled to
WebAssembly via \texttt{wasm-bindgen}, registering 60 process mining algorithms
in a kernel registry: discovery (directly-follows graphs, Heuristic
Miner~\cite{weijters_et_al_heuristics_miner_2006}, Inductive
Miner~\cite{leemans_fahland_van_der_aalst_2013_constructive}, POWL
2.0~\cite{kourani_van_der_aalst_powl2_2024}), conformance checking (token
replay~\cite{rozinat_van_der_aalst_2008}, alignments), object-centric analysis
(OCEL~2.0 ingestion, OC-DFG~\cite{berti_van_der_aalst_oc_dfg_2023}, OCPQ
querying~\cite{kuesters_van_der_aalst_ocpq_2025}), and supporting ML primitives.
The \textbf{orchestration layer} is a TypeScript monorepo of eleven packages
providing the \texttt{wpm} CLI, Zod-validated configuration, an execution
planner with four deployment profiles, OpenTelemetry observability, and a
Supabase-backed receipt store with an offline sync queue (five-attempt cap,
dead-letter table) for intermittently connected environments.

The WASM compilation target is itself a security decision: the admission gate
runs in a sandboxed, capability-free module with no ambient filesystem or
network authority, embeddable in browsers, server runtimes, and edge platforms
--- so the verifier executes \emph{inside the data owner's boundary}, and only
hashes and receipts leave it.

\subsection{Determinism as a Receipt Precondition}
\label{sec:impl-determinism}

Lemma~\ref{lem:chain} requires that any holder of a receipt can recompute its
hashes. This is only possible if the underlying computation is reproducible:
a non-deterministic analysis yields different \texttt{output\_hash} values on
different machines, making every receipt unverifiable. wasm4pm therefore
enforces bit-exact determinism as a CI merge gate --- identical input must
produce identical output bytes across platforms. Concretely: all randomness is
seeded (stochastic algorithms currently use a fixed seed), all hash-map
iteration is order-sorted before serialisation, and floating-point operations
avoid platform-dependent intrinsics. Canonicalisation of OCEL~2.0 documents
follows a JCS-style profile (UTF-16 lexicographic key ordering, deterministic
array ordering) so that hash computation is byte-stable regardless of producer
formatting --- the implementation of Definition~\ref{def:valid-receipt}(i).

\subsection{TrueX Envelopes as Concrete Admitted Changes}
\label{sec:impl-truex}

The \emph{TrueX envelope} realises a candidate change
$x \in \Omega_{\mathrm{change}}$ on the wire.
Table~\ref{tab:envelope} maps envelope fields to the formal components of
Definition~\ref{def:omega}.

\begin{table}[t]

\caption{TrueX envelope fields and their formal counterparts.}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{llX}
\toprule
\textbf{Envelope field} & \textbf{Formal component} & \textbf{Content}\\
\midrule
\texttt{session\_id}          & $a$ (actor/session)     & originating session identity\\
\texttt{ocel2\_batch\_hash}   & $h(O)$                  & BLAKE3 over canonical OCEL~2.0 batch\\
\texttt{receipt\_hash}        & $h(R)$                  & BLAKE3 over the receipt body\\

\end{table}

Two architectural rules protect the envelope's integrity. First,
\texttt{admission\_status} is \emph{verifier-written}: the producer cannot set
it, and a producer-supplied alignment claim triggers the
\texttt{SelfCertifiedAlignment} refusal --- the implementation of
Definition~\ref{def:valid-receipt}(iii). Second, ingestion is idempotent on
\texttt{receipt\_hash}: replayed envelopes are detected at the persistence
boundary, complementing the nonce check of C5.

\subsection{The Boundary Predicate as a Refusal Taxonomy}
\label{sec:impl-refusals}

The admission-time checks of Definitions~\ref{def:valid-receipt}
and~\ref{def:accept} are implemented as thirteen non-overlapping refusal
states, each a \textsc{deny}-level verdict with a structured payload (code,
severity, JSON path, message):

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item[Ingress validation:] \texttt{Observed\-OCEL\-Missing},
        \texttt{Expected\-OCEL\-Missing}, \texttt{Path\-Hash\-Only\-Receipt}
        --- the substance axiom; a receipt without recomputable logs is
        refused at the door.
  \item[Adversarial detection:]
        \texttt{Observed\-Trace\-Mutation\-Without\-Boundary},
        \texttt{Fixture\-Mutation\-Detected} --- structural-isomorphism and
        temporal-offset analysis catches near-clones of golden fixtures whose
        timestamps and identifiers were shifted.
  \item[Envelope verification:] \texttt{RuntimeObserverMissing} --- receipts
        claiming live execution must carry the runner envelope (runner ID,
        device, build, session nonces, timestamps).
  \item[Cryptographic binding:] \texttt{Challenge\-Nonce\-Missing},
        \texttt{Challenge\-Nonce\-Mismatch},
        \texttt{Observed\-Trace\-Not\-Challenge\-Bound}
        --- the pre-minted 128-bit nonce must appear in the challenge manifest,
        the raw runner output, \emph{and} the observed OCEL events
        (Definition~\ref{def:valid-receipt}(iv)).
  \item[Evidence binding:] \texttt{BoundaryEvidenceMissing},
        \texttt{SummaryOnlyReceipt} --- raw executor evidence (exit codes,
        stdout/stderr hashes) must be bound, and observed logs must contain
        granular provenance events, not domain-level summaries.
  \item[Authority \& hierarchy:] \texttt{SelfCertifiedAlignment},
        \texttt{ProofClassOverclaimed} --- alignment is verifier-sealed, and no
        receipt may claim a proof class whose conditions it does not meet.
\end{description}

The taxonomy's closure property matters for the implementation-defect channel
of Theorem~\ref{thm:decomp}: because refusals are enumerable, ``is every
failure mode handled?'' becomes a finite checklist, each entry with dedicated
negative tests that inject the corresponding forged receipt and assert
refusal.

\subsection{Evaluation}
\label{sec:impl-eval}

\paragraph{Proof gates.} Every commit to the repository runs a proof-gate
suite of 16 receipt-backed tests across seven proof files (receipt
construction and hashing, release admissibility, WASM module surface, OCEL
end-to-end validation, ML primitives, statistical process control, and
reinforcement-learning components). The gate is enforced in the pre-push hook
and CI with a hard time budget (the current suite completes in under 1.5\,s),
so admission-relevant regressions cannot land silently. In the terminology of
Section~\ref{sec:bg-receipts}, the suite maintains \textsc{ChicagoProof}
status for the core pipeline: at least one test exercises the real WASM
boundary end-to-end, and mocking that boundary is prohibited by policy and
enforced by review tooling.

\paragraph{Determinism.} The merge gate re-executes representative analyses
and compares output bytes; a single differing byte fails the build. This has
held across the browser and Node.js targets of the WASM artifact.

\paragraph{Footprint.} The full browser-profile WASM binary is
$\approx 3.5$\,MB (with a CI size-regression gate at $+5\%$); size-reduced
profiles ($\approx 0.5$--$2$\,MB) target mobile, IoT, edge, and fog
deployments. The practical consequence for the framework is deployment
locality: the admission gate is small enough to run at the point of change ---
in the browser form, at the API gateway, on the shop-floor gateway --- rather
than as a remote service the data must travel to.

\paragraph{Operational verification.} The \texttt{wpm doctor} protocol
verifies the admission pipeline itself and reports a three-state progression:
\texttt{prepublish\_only} (credentials absent or store unreachable),
\texttt{configured} (reachable, migrations applied, service role present), and
\texttt{live\_verified}. The last is earned by a live probe that exercises
every leg of the evidence path --- upsert a probe receipt, read it back, write
a dead-letter entry, ingest a TrueX envelope through the edge function --- and
then emits a BLAKE3-hashed \emph{runtime receipt} recording the probe results.
The status is thus itself an instance of the framework: the claim ``the
admission pipeline works'' is only accepted when accompanied by a verifiable
receipt, closing the self-reference loop.

\paragraph{Limitations of the current implementation.} Three gaps between the
formal model and the shipped system are tracked openly: ML-DSA signing is
specified but the deployed chain currently relies on BLAKE3 integrity plus
transport authentication, with signature integration staged behind the
crypto-agility interface of Section~\ref{sec:channels}; CLI-side and WASM-side
OpenTelemetry traces are not yet correlated into a single distributed trace;
and stochastic algorithm seeds are fixed rather than configurable-and-audited.
None of these affect the validity of the propositions --- they delimit which
proof classes the current artifacts can claim.
