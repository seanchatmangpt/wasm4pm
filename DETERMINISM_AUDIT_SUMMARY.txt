================================================================================
CRITICAL FINDINGS
================================================================================

1. ISSUE: HashMap in streaming_dfg.rs (line 62)
   ───────────────────────────────────────────
   File: wasm4pm/src/streaming/streaming_dfg.rs
   Code: pub open_traces: HashMap<String, Vec<u32>>
   Risk: Case ID iteration order is random
   Impact: HIGH — All streaming algorithms affected
   Fix: Sort case_ids before iterating
   Effort: 10 minutes

2. ISSUE: Unseeded fastrand in playout.rs
   ──────────────────────────────────────
   File: wasm4pm/src/playout.rs
   Code: fastrand::usize(..children.len()), fastrand::f64()
   Risk: Each run produces different trace (Monte Carlo simulation)
   Impact: MEDIUM — Simulation results not reproducible
   Fix: Accept seed parameter, pass to fastrand::Rng::with_seed(seed)
   Effort: 20 minutes

3. ISSUE: Hardcoded Seeds in Stochastic Algorithms
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────
   File: wasm4pm/src/genetic_discovery.rs (and others)
   Code: let mut rng = StdRng::seed_from_u64(42);
   Risk: No way for caller to reproduce specific random variant
   Impact: MEDIUM — Research reproducibility, testing flexibility
   Fix: Expose seed as optional WASM parameter (default 42)
   Effort: 1-2 hours
