\chapter*{Abstract}
Software engineering has traditionally separated compilation from process enforcement, resulting in a systemic divergence between organizational intention and operational reality. This dissertation resolves this divergence by establishing a unified field theory of Process Intelligence and Operational Physics. We define the mathematical frameworks of the Impossibility Gradient and Impossibility Harvesting, proving that structural and typestate validation failures can be leveraged to extract absolute compliance guarantees. Utilizing \texttt{wasm4pm}, \texttt{cargo-cicd}, and \texttt{chicago-tdd-tools}, we construct a zero-trust, self-healing execution environment where WebAssembly compliance courts adjudicate process traces against formal Petri nets and POWL models. Finally, we formulate Chatman's Law of Organizational Work, establishing the thermodynamic boundaries of software development pipelines.
