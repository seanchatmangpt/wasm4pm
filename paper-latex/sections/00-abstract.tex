This monograph presents the formal mathematical foundations and architectural thesis of \code{wasm4pm}, an autonomic process mining system built entirely on a WebAssembly (WASM) kernel. Deviating from traditional, unbounded intelligence architectures, \code{wasm4pm} enforces a strict ``Blue-Ocean Thesis'' targeting First-Mile Mechanical Intelligence. By treating process state transformations as strict algebraic invariants executing within the sovereign sanctuary of linear memory, the system achieves microsecond bounds on execution, provable idempotency, and deterministic auditability. We detail the advanced algebraic, geometric, and discrete calculus models governing 60 kernel algorithms and 55 cognitive breeds, substantiating every claim with cryptographically bound execution receipts.
